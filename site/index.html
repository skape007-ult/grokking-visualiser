What am I looking at?

Grokking is when a neural network, long after it has perfectly memorised its training examples, suddenly starts getting examples it has never seen right. For a long stretch it looks like nothing is happening: training accuracy is already 100% and accuracy on new examples is near zero. Then, with no change to the setup, it "gets it", and accuracy on new examples shoots up to 100%.

What was done. The task is to answer (a + b) mod 97 for every pair of numbers from 0 to 96: 9,409 sums in all. A small transformer (one layer, about 220,000 parameters) was shown 30% of them (2,823 sums) and trained for 25,000 steps, then tested on the remaining 70% it had never seen. Training used the AdamW optimiser with weight decay 1.0, a penalty that constantly nudges the network's weights towards zero. Following Nanda et al., the model deliberately has no LayerNorm, which makes it easier to interpret. Each other setting changes exactly one thing, and every setting was trained with three different random seeds.

What the toggle shows. In standard training (bold lines, seed 0) the network hits 99% on its training examples by step 130, but only crosses 95% on unseen examples at step 9,868, 76× later. The timing depends on the seed: in the other two (faint lines) it came at steps 5,785 and 4,216. The delay is real every time, but its length varies by more than 2×. Remove weight decay and generalisation never came, in any of the three seeds: they memorise just as fast, yet after 25,000 steps they are right on only 3–4% of unseen examples, and the embeddings never form rings. With twice the data (60%) there is barely a delay: 95% on unseen examples by step 269–489. With half the data (15%), unseen accuracy never rose above 2% (chance is 1%) in any seed. Those runs may still grok later, but not within this training budget.

Limits. This is one task (addition mod 97), one small architecture and one 25,000-step budget. Three seeds per setting is enough to show the effect isn't a fluke of one run, but not enough to pin down how the timing is distributed. "Never" means "not within 25,000 steps". The embedding panel squeezes 128 dimensions into 2, and those two directions capture under a third of the variance. It shows the dominant structure, not everything the network has learned.

Papers: Power et al. (2022), Grokking: Generalization Beyond Overfitting on Small Algorithmic Datasets; Nanda et al. (2023), Progress measures for grokking via mechanistic interpretability. Code and data: REPO_URL.
